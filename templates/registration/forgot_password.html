Password Recovery Assistance

PUCIT Class Management System

Self-Service Recovery Notice: For student account security, self-service email recovery has been disabled. If you have forgotten your password, your Class Representative (CR) or Course Administrator can issue a secure temporary password.

How to Reset Your Password:

  1. Contact your designated Class Representative (CR) or Course Administrator in person or via official university channels.
  2. Provide your university Roll Number (e.g., BITF24A001) and confirm your identity.
  3. Your administrator will perform an assisted reset and securely provide you with a one-time temporary password.
  4. Upon logging in with the temporary password, the CMS will immediately prompt you to set your new private password.
